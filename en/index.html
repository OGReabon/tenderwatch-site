Questions

Which tenders do you cover?

Every call for tenders published on TED, the EU's official procurement journal — all above-threshold public contracts in the EU and EEA. National below-threshold portals are not covered yet.

Can I see a sample first?

Yes. Email info@tenderwatching.com with what your company does and which countries you cover, and we'll send a free sample with this week's matches.

Which language is the email in?

English or Czech, your choice. The tender's category is shown in your language and the link opens the notice in that language on TED. The title itself stays in the buyer's language — Czech for Czech tenders, German for Austrian ones, and so on.

How do I cancel?

Reply "cancel" to any email. You won't be charged again.
